WHO WE ARE

TRUSTED AUTOMOTIVE SOLUTIONS SINCE 2020

A LEGACY OF RELIABILITY

Established in 1980, Pathirana Motors Store (Pvt) Ltd has quickly risen to become a premier supplier of light and heavy vehicle machinery parts in Sri Lanka. Based in the heart of Colombo's automotive hub, Panchikawatta, we serve as a one-stop center for all your mechanical needs.

We specialize in both new and refurbished high-performance components, ensuring that logistics and industrial sectors keep moving without delay. Our dedication to precision engineering has made us a trusted partner for national institutions.

OUR MISSION

Following the standards of industry leaders, we strive to become a market leader by providing high-quality products with unparalleled customer support.
